FEATURE 08 — MITIGATION & PROTECTION

Automatic threat mitigation.

When a threat is confirmed, PhishGuard takes immediate action to protect the user.

🚨
Warning Banner Injection
A full-width alert banner is injected at the top of the malicious page, warning the user before they interact with any content.
🔴
Badge Indicator Update
The extension icon badge changes to red "!!" for critical threats, amber "!" for moderate, or green "✓" for safe — visible at a glance.
🔔
Desktop Notification
Critical threats trigger a desktop notification with the threat level, predicted effects, and confidence score — even if the popup isn't open.
📊
Threat History Logging
Every analysis is logged with full feature data, prediction results, and timestamps — creating an auditable record of detected threats.
🛡️
Safe Page Verification
Trusted pages receive a green shield indicator with confidence score, giving users visible confirmation that the page is safe to use.
💡
Context-Specific Risk Guidance
Users receive actionable guidance: "Do not enter credentials" for credential theft, "Do not download" for malware, or "Proceed with caution" for low risk.
🔍
Real-Time Page Monitoring
Content scripts continuously monitor for DOM changes, new forms, and dynamic redirects — catching threats that appear after page load.
⛔
Block Site Button
For critical threats, the popup offers a "Block Site" button that prevents further interaction with the domain during the session.
